Women Safety Protocols

Essential Tactical Defense & Emergency Guidelines

1. Physical Defense & Vulnerable Target Points
  • Target Vulnerable Areas: In physical conflict, aim directly for sensitive areas: Eyes (poke), Nose (palm strike upward), Throat (jab), Groin (kick/knee), and Instep (heel stamp).
  • Key Weapon Technique: Hold house/car keys protruding between fingers firmly when walking alone at night.
  • Break Wrist Grips: Rotate wrist towards the opponent's thumb joint to break free instantly.
2. Public Transport & Commute Protocol
  • Live Location Sharing: Send live location to family before boarding Rickshaw, CNG, or Uber.
  • Fake Call Technique: Speak vehicle license plate number loudly on a fake call if suspicious.
  • Aisle Seating Rule: Sit near exits in buses/trains. Avoid isolated back corners.
3. Cyber Safety & Stalking Safeguards
  • Police Cyber Support for Women: Call 01769-690033 or email cyberwomen@police.gov.bd.
  • Preserve Evidence: Take full screenshots showing timestamps and URLs before reporting.
  • Enable 2FA: Secure all social accounts with Two-Factor Authentication.
