Self Defence Awareness

Essential Women Safety Guidelines

1. Situational Awareness

Always stay mindful of your surroundings. Avoid looking at mobile phones continuously while walking in quiet or unlit streets.

2. Vulnerable Target Points

If attacked, aim for critical sensitive areas: Eyes, Nose, Throat, Solar Plexus, and Groin. Use maximum force immediately.

3. Everyday Items as Tools

Keys held between fingers, heavy bags, pens, pepper sprays, or umbrella tips can serve as effective immediate defense tools.

4. Loud Vocal Reaction

Shout words like "FIRE" or "HELP" loudly. A firm loud voice startles attackers and instantly alerts nearby citizens.
